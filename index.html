How it works

Story first, then skill, then celebration

  1. 1

    Hear the story

    Each game opens with a short, faithful retelling of a Bible passage, written for young readers.

  2. 2

    Practise the skill

    Children solve maths problems or word puzzles woven into the story, with hints when they need them.

  3. 3

    Reflect & celebrate

    Finish with a memory verse, a star reward and a simple question to talk about at home or in class.
